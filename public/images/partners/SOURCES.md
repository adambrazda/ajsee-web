# Partner logo provenance

Audited on 2026-10-09. The four newly selected Ticketmaster and TodayTix SVGs are copied byte for byte from official public brand archives. smsticket PNGs and ColosseumTicket SVGs were compared byte for byte with their original source files. No paths, colours, proportions or trademark symbols were edited. Dark backgrounds use supplied white/reversed artwork; no CSS recolouring is applied.

## Ticketmaster — official public brand archive

- Brand page: https://business.ticketmaster.cz/logo-guide/
- Archive linked by that page: https://business.ticketmaster.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Ticketmaster-Logo-With-Registered-Trademark-Symbol.zip
- `ticketmaster.svg`: `Ticketmaster Logo With Registered Trademark Symbol/RGB/svg/Ticketmaster-Logo-Dark-RGB.svg` (Cosmos Black, includes registered trademark symbol).
- `ticketmaster-dark.svg`: `Ticketmaster Logo With Registered Trademark Symbol/RGB/svg/Ticketmaster-Logo-White-RGB.svg`.
- The logo slot reserves at least 12px of horizontal clear space, in addition to vertical space around the contained artwork.

## smsticket — official public logo manual archive

- Official page linking the Logo Manual: https://www.smsticket.cz/pro-poradatele
- Archive: https://www.smsticket.cz/logomanual.zip
- `smsticket.png`: `smsticket ruzne obrazky na web/smsticket - predprodej vstupenek_modre logo, bez pozadi.png`.

## ColosseumTicket — original official website assets

- Both URLs are referenced in https://www.colosseumticket.cz/cs.
- `colosseumticket.svg`: https://www.datocms-assets.com/31357/1595430365-colosseum-logo-dark.svg
- `colosseumticket-dark.svg`: https://www.datocms-assets.com/31357/1594982651-colosseum-logo-white.svg
- A separate public marketing archive was not located. These are verified original website assets, not a claimed public marketing kit.

## TrustedStays — existing files, archive provenance not verified

- `trustedstays/TrustedStays_Primary_RGB_R.svg` and `trustedstays/TrustedStays_Reversed_RGB_R.svg` already existed in AJSEE and are unchanged.
- The visual identity matches the official TrustedStays website, but the source of these exact SVG exports has not been established. Do not describe these specific files as verified downloads from a public marketing pack.
- Official website: https://www.trustedstays.co.uk/
- The current public website PNG contains an additional partnership tagline and is not byte-identical to AJSEE's existing PNG/SVG variants.
- Their published terms state that brand guidelines are supplied on request: https://www.trustedstays.co.uk/terms-of-use.pdf, section 7 of the Acceptable Use Policy (page 11).
